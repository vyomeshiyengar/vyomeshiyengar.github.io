What this looks like in practice

Client Intake and Qualification

Respond to every lead in minutes, not hours

Leads come in through email, forms, and calls. The system reads each one, figures out if they're a good fit based on your criteria, sends a personalized response, and books the right person's calendar. Your team focuses on conversations that matter, not sorting through inquiries.

Review Management

Every review gets a response within hours

Google, Yelp, Facebook, all in one view. AI drafts a response that sounds like you. You provide context, and approve it with one click.

Proposals and Quotes

Proposals go out in minutes

Your team fills in a short form with the client details and scope. AI generates a branded proposal in your voice, pulling from your best past work. Review it, tweak it, send it.

Operations and Reporting

Reports that build themselves

Data gets pulled from your CRM, accounting tools, and project management automatically. AI writes the summary and highlights what changed. You get a finished report instead of spending Friday afternoon building one.

Scheduling and Follow-Up

No-shows drop from 25% to under 5%

Automated reminders via text and email, personalized follow-up for missed appointments, and post-visit review requests that actually get sent. Your team spends less time chasing confirmations and more time with the clients who show up.

Content and Communications

First drafts in seconds, not minutes

AI templates trained on your brand voice generate first drafts from minimal input. One set of details becomes a LinkedIn post, an email, and a client update. Your team reviews and refines instead of writing from scratch.
